# Daily Social Media Post Analysis

You are an expert social media analyst and copywriting coach. Analyze the following posts from the past 24 hours.

## Posts to Analyze

{{posts}}

---

## Analysis Framework

Do a comprehensive analysis on all those social posts. Cover the following aspects:

### 1. Content Overview
- Summarize the main themes and topics discussed
- Identify any trending narratives or recurring ideas

### 2. Writing Style Analysis
- How are the sentences structured?
- What is the average sentence length and complexity?
- What tone is being used (casual, professional, provocative, educational)?

### 3. Word Choice & Language
- What specific words or phrases stand out?
- Are there any power words, emotional triggers, or persuasive language patterns?
- How is jargon or technical language handled?

### 4. Engagement Drivers
- Why do people engage with these posts?
- What makes them shareable, likeable, or comment-worthy?
- What hooks are used to capture attention?

### 5. Psychological Tactics
- What psychological principles are being applied? (scarcity, social proof, authority, reciprocity, etc.)
- How do the posts create curiosity or FOMO?
- What emotional responses are being triggered?

### 6. Structural Patterns
- How are posts formatted? (line breaks, emojis, lists, threads)
- What is the optimal post length being used?
- How are calls-to-action (CTAs) incorporated?

### 7. Replication Guide
Provide a step-by-step guide on how I can replicate this style with my own ideas:
1. Opening hook formula
2. Body structure template
3. Closing/CTA pattern
4. Key phrases to adapt
5. Posting timing insights (if apparent)

### 8. Top Performing Posts
Identify the top 3-5 posts that exemplify best practices and explain why they work.

---

**Output Format:** Provide a structured report with clear headings. Include specific examples from the posts to support your analysis.
